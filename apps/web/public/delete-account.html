Delete your Maine Farm Market account

Maine Farm Market, operated by CAC-USA Developers LLC (in formation), lets you permanently delete your account from either the Android app or the website.

Delete while signed in

  1. Sign in to Maine Farm Market.
  2. Open Account and safety.
  3. Type DELETE and select Permanently delete my account.
Open Account and safety

Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, email mainefarmmarket@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. Use the subject “Maine Farm Market account deletion request.” We may ask you to verify account ownership.

What is deleted or retained

We delete your sign-in account, profile, cart, listings, farm profile, listing images, and blocked-account list. Producer subscriptions are canceled. Most data is removed promptly and within 30 days. Transaction, fraud-prevention, tax, payment, safety, and legal records may be retained for up to seven years and are disconnected from your public profile where practical.

See the Maine Farm Market Privacy Policy.
